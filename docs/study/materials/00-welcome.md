# Welcome

Thank you for being here. The session takes about two hours.

## What you will do

- Work on a small program you have never seen before. Someone built it over
  six weeks and then left.
- Handle a few requests that have come in about it.
- Do this twice, with two different setups for looking at the project's
  history, on two different projects.

We are comparing the two setups. We are not testing you. If something confuses
you, that is the most useful thing you can tell us.

## Please think out loud

- Say what you are about to do, and what you expect to happen.
- When something surprises you, say what you expected instead.
- When you do not understand what you are looking at, say so.

We record the screen and audio. You can ask us to stop at any time.

## What you can use

- Any tool on the machine.
- An AI coding assistant, already open and ready. Ask it for anything,
  including running commands. Use it as much or as little as you normally
  would.

## How the time breaks down

| Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|
| 10 | A few questions about your background |
| 10 | We walk you through the first setup on a practice project |
| 45 | The requests |
| 8 | Two short questionnaires and a few questions |
| 10 | Same walkthrough again with the other setup |
| 45 | The requests on the other project |
| 8 | Questionnaires again |
| 15 | We talk about how it went |

Each request has a time limit.

- Stopping in the middle is fine.
- Finishing early is fine.
- We expect people to run out of time on some of them. That is a normal
  result, not a problem.

## One thing to know

Nothing you do can break anything that matters. Every project is a fresh copy.
If you get one into a state you cannot get out of, say so and we will reset it.
Getting stuck is information for us, so please do not hide it.
